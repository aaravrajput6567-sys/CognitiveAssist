Why Traditional Brain Games Fail Seniors

Standard puzzles and digital games were never built for cognitive impairment. They cause agitation, lack personal meaning, and leave doctors completely in the dark.

❌ Traditional Brain Apps

The Three Frustrating Pitfalls

  • ✕

    Static, Punishing Challenge Levels

    Games don't notice when an elderly player is exhausted or eye-strained. Strict timers induce panic, agitation, and early abandonment.

  • ✕

    Generic, Meaningless Puzzles

    Stock crosswords and abstract numbers fail to evoke emotional memory, ignoring proven autobiographical reminiscence pathways.

  • ✕

    Caregiver & Doctor Blind Spots

    Checkups occur every 6–12 months. Families have no objective day-to-day data to detect insidious motor-cognitive micro-declines.

A local heuristic generator is also included so the system never fails even if an API key is absent.

The CRI is an evidence-based composite metric (scored 0–100) formulated specifically for Mild Cognitive Impairment. It balances Recall Accuracy (45%), Motor Reaction Latency in milliseconds (35%), and Cognitive Endurance/Fatigue Resilience (20%). It allows neurologists to distinguish between transient tiredness and sustained cognitive change.
